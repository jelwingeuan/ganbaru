## Q1. What jlpt.jp offers for free download: sample questions, the Official Practice Workbook (公式問題集) volumes, listening MP3s and scripts

### Takeaway
jlpt.jp hosts four sets of free files. The most important are the complete contents of both Official Practice Workbooks, the 2012 『日本語能力試験公式問題集』 and the 2018 『同 第二集』, for every level N1 to N5. Each level comes as test booklets in PDF, listening MP3s, an answer sheet, an answer key and a listening script, which is roughly one full real test per level per volume. There is no third volume. The other free sets are the 2009 "New JLPT Sample Questions" collection (問題例集; PDF and MP3), interactive one-question-per-item-type pages for N1 to N5, and teacher slide ZIPs. Braille versions of the 2012 workbook and the 2009 collection are also posted. Downloading for personal study is free and needs no registration.

### Cited Findings

**A. Official Practice Workbook, Vol. 1 (2012) and Vol. 2 (2018): free full PDFs and MP3s**
- Landing pages: English https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html and Japanese https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sampleindex.html. Both language pages link to the same files under `https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sample2012/` and `.../sample2018/`. — [JLPT Official Practice Workbook (EN)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html); [公式問題集 (JA)](https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sampleindex.html)
- jlpt.jp says that since the 2010 revision two workbooks have been released: one in 2012 and Vol. 2 in 2018. Each holds, for every level, about one real test's worth of questions selected from tests actually given after 2010. In other words, these are official past-test questions (過去問), not newly written mock items. — [EN page](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html); [JA page](https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sampleindex.html)
- The Japanese page still says two volumes have been published so far, and the braille page says the same. — [JA page](https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sampleindex.html); [点字ファイル](https://www.jlpt.jp/tenji.html)
- Files per level: 4 PDF test booklets (V = vocabulary, G = grammar, R = reading, L = listening). N1 and N2 have two test sections: Language Knowledge plus Reading, and Listening. N3 to N5 have three: Vocabulary, Grammar plus Reading, and Listening. Each level also has 4–5 MP3s, one per listening item type, and 3 extra PDFs: a sample answer sheet (解答用紙), an answer key (正答表) and a listening script (聴解スクリプト). — [EN page](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html)
- Server Last-Modified dates: all 2012 files 14 Jun 2012, all 2018 files 27 Dec 2018, and the "sample2017" replacement MP3s 10 May 2017. — HEAD requests to the URLs below
- **Size discrepancy 1:** for the 2018 PDFs, both the English and Japanese pages print exactly the same sizes as for the 2012 PDFs. The server sizes of the 2018 files are different (see tables), so the 2018 sizes on the page look like a copy-paste error. — [EN page](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html) compared with HEAD Content-Length
- **Size discrepancy 2:** the 2012 edition's Q2 listening links (N1–N5) point to files in a `sample2017/` folder, which are 8.5–15.2 MB. The page still prints the old 2.6–4.5 MB sizes, which match the original `sample2012/mp3/N?Q2.mp3` files that are still on the server. — HEAD requests
- **Complete workbook file list with URLs and sizes (2018 and 2012 editions)**: each table compares the size printed on the page with the server's Content-Length. Source: [EN workbook page](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html), [JA workbook page](https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sampleindex.html) and HEAD requests on 26 Oct 2026.

## Q3. Copyright and terms of use on jlpt.jp: is redistribution allowed, and can a third-party app quote or adapt questions?

### Takeaway
The jlpt.jp Site Policy, section 1 (Copyright), sets the following rules:
- Personal study use is free, including copying, adapting and translating.
- Quotation is allowed under strict conditions, with a source credit.
- Copying for classes at non-profit schools is allowed.
- Reprint and reproduction for Japanese-language education is pre-authorized only when no fee is charged for the resulting materials, again with a source credit.
- Everything else needs prior written permission from JF and JEES.

Rights that belong to third parties are excluded from all of these allowances and need the rights holder's own permission. That covers all N1–N5 listening audio and specific N1/N2 grammar and reading passages. Redistribution of the official files is not generally allowed. A commercial app may not reuse or adapt official questions without permission.

### Cited Findings
- Site Policy URLs: [EN](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html) and [JA](https://www.jlpt.jp/policy.html). The site footer carries a copyright notice dated 2012 naming the Japan Foundation and JEES. — [EN policy](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html)
- **General rule.** Site works and the site as a whole (as a compilation) are protected under Japanese copyright law and the Berne Convention. Copying, public transmission (公衆送信) and adaptation without permission are prohibited, except within the scope JF/JEES grant or where the law allows. Content may not be altered against the author's will. — [Site Policy §1 (JA)](https://www.jlpt.jp/policy.html)
- **(1) Personal use.** All or part of the works may be reproduced, copied, adapted and translated without permission "for personal use such as individual study". — [Site Policy §1(1) (EN)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html)
- **(2) Quotation.** Quoting is allowed without permission if all of these hold:
  - The amount quoted is reasonable and only what is needed, and any summary is faithful.
  - The quoted part is clearly marked off, for example in brackets or a box.
  - The user's own work is the main part and the quote is subordinate.
  - A source credit is placed next to the quote, naming the Official Worldwide JLPT Website and https://www.jlpt.jp/, prefixed with 出典/提供 or Source/Copyright.

  — [Site Policy §1(2)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html)
- **(3) Schools.** Teachers and students at non-profit educational institutions may copy works as needed for a class, with a source credit and without unreasonably harming the rights holder. — [Site Policy §1(3)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html)
- **(4) Free educational materials.** Outside cases (1)–(3), JF and JEES grant permission in advance to reprint and reproduce (JA: 転載・複製) works for Japanese-language education. This applies only when no fee is charged for the teaching materials produced, and a source credit is required. Summaries must be faithful. Items carrying a no-reproduction notice go through route (5). — [Site Policy §1(4) (JA)](https://www.jlpt.jp/policy.html)
- **Third-party exclusion.** Parts of the sample questions contain third-party works, and using those parts needs separate permission from the rights holder. This covers the 2009 collection, the 2012 workbook and the 2018 Vol. 2, and applies under (4) and (5) alike. The affected items are marked on the workbook page, and the organizers will help find unknown rights holders via Inquiries. — [Site Policy §1(4)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html)
- **(5) Everything else.** Apply in advance via Inquiries (https://www.jlpt.jp/e/contact/index.html) and get JF/JEES permission. The request must state:
  - purpose and form of use
  - media and page count
  - author, and publisher with contact details
  - estimated retail price and number of copies
  - an outline of the publication
  - which part of the site will be used, and the period of use

  — [Site Policy §1(5)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html)
- **Third-party items flagged on the workbook page.** For all of these, unauthorized reprint or reproduction is prohibited except under policy (1)–(3).
  - 2018 Vol. 2: N1 Q7, Q8(1)(3)(4), Q9(1)–(3), Q10, Q11, Q12; N2 Q10(1)(3)(5), Q11(1)–(3), Q13; and all listening audio files, N1–N5.
  - 2012 edition: N1 Q7, Q8(1)–(3), Q9(1)–(3), Q10, Q11, Q12; N2 Q10(3)–(5), Q11(1)–(3), Q13; and all listening audio, N1–N5.
  - The JA page also cites an Asahi Shimbun permission number (22-1573) for the 2012 set.

  — [Workbook page (EN)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html); [JA](https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sampleindex.html)
- **Links.** Linking is welcome without prior notice, but the link must say it goes to the official JLPT site. The site must not be set up to appear embedded inside another website. — [Site Policy §2](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/policy.html)
- **Leaked questions.** Questions and answers from real tests are not public, and using leaked material is sanctioned. See Q2. — [2024 warning](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/topics/202401191708325175.html)

### Inferences (my reading, not legal advice)
- **Personal study.** The user may download, print, annotate, and translate all the files in Q1 for their own study.
- **Redistribution.** Re-hosting or bundling the PDFs and MP3s in an app or website is reproduction plus public transmission, so it is not covered by (1)–(3). The listening audio is excluded in every case because of third-party rights.
- **Free app with no charges.** A non-commercial Japanese-education app might fit (4) for JF/JEES-owned text questions, provided it credits the source. But (4) mentions only 転載・複製 (reprint and reproduction) and does not mention adaptation (翻案). Converting questions into new formats, editing them or translating them for public use would likely need (5) permission.
- **Commercial app.** An app with subscriptions, in-app purchases or possibly ads arguably charges a fee, which takes it outside (4). Any reuse or adaptation of official questions in such an app would need prior (5) permission.
- **Safest path for a study web app:**
  - Link out to the official PDF and MP3 URLs, opening them on jlpt.jp rather than embedding or framing them.
  - Write original questions that follow the publicly described item types.
  - Use only short, credited quotations under (2) where needed, for example when explaining an item type.
  - Apply via the Inquiries form before reusing any official question text.

### Gaps
- The policy says nothing specific about software apps, ad-supported free services, or AI training data. There is also no published turnaround time or fee for (5) permissions.
- It is unclear whether hot-linking an MP3 into an in-app audio player counts as the embedding that §2 forbids. Ask JF/JEES.

## Q4. Widely used unofficial mock tests and practice sources, and their licensing

### Takeaway
None of the popular third-party sources is openly licensed. Commercial book series such as Shin Kanzen Master, the 3A mock-test books and Sou Matome are ordinary all-rights-reserved publications. Many include one full mock test. Web and app services publish their own copyrighted questions under their terms of service: JLPT Sensei is free to download, Bunpro is subscription-based, and Migii and Japanesetest4you are freemium or free sites. Their questions can be used for personal study but not reused in another app without a licence. Several "free JLPT PDF" sites have unclear provenance or are pirated.

### Cited Findings
- **JLPT Sensei (jlptsensei.com).** Offers free downloadable practice tests per level; the site navigation lists a "Practice Test – Free Download" for N5, N4, N3 and others. The tests copy the JLPT section structure and time limits, and the site describes them as based on the actual test. They are not official JF/JEES material. ## Q5. Are there open-licensed (CC) JLPT-style question banks an app could legally reuse?

### Takeaway
I found no openly licensed (CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0) bank of JLPT-style exam questions. Open data does exist for vocabulary, kanji and example sentences, which an app can use to write its own original questions:
- OpenJLPT (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- JMdict and KANJIDIC from EDRDG (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- Tatoeba sentences (CC BY 2.0 FR by default)
- Jonathan Waller's Tanos JLPT lists (reported as CC BY, unverified)

The ShareAlike and attribution obligations must be respected.

### Cited Findings
- **OpenJLPT.** Version 0.1.0 on PyPI, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, repository https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT. It provides JLPT N5–N1 vocabulary (8,334 words) and kanji (2,211) as JSON, CSV and SQLite. Tatoeba example sentences cover 89% of the words, and grammar is listed as coming soon. It contains no exam questions. — [PyPI JSON metadata and README](https://pypi.org/project/openjlpt/)
- **JMdict, KANJIDIC and other EDRDG dictionary files.** Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 under the EDRDG General Dictionary Licence. — [EDRDG licence statement](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html)
- **Tatoeba.** Sentences are CC BY 2.0 FR by default, so attribution is required. Audio may be under other licences, including some limited to use on Tatoeba itself. — [Tatoeba Terms of Use §6](https://tatoeba.org/en/terms_of_use)
- **Tanos JLPT lists (Jonathan Waller).** Credited as Creative Commons Attribution by apps that reuse them. The source site tanos.co.uk did not respond, so this is unverified at the source. — [kumi.app attributions](https://kumi.app/attributions); [Japanese.io data sources](https://www.japanese.io/sources/)
- Other "JLPT kanji" repositories on GitHub say they inherit the licences of the upstream dictionary files, mainly EDRDG CC BY-SA. — [jlpt_kanji_json_msgpack](https://github.com/Smallsan/jlpt_kanji_json_msgpack)
- Hugging Face results were general Japanese NLU benchmarks (JGLUE) or gated datasets (llm-jp/jhle), not JLPT question banks. — [JGLUE README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/llm-book/JGLUE/blame/cf4f9d80e201fd1cedd82ebb4c560976196c6bbb/README.md); [llm-jp/jhle](https://huggingface.co/datasets/llm-jp/jhle)

### Inferences
- The legally clean route for a JLPT study app is to write original questions in the official item types, using open vocabulary, kanji and sentence data, and to credit those sources. Then link out to jlpt.jp for the official workbooks.
- CC BY-SA data (OpenJLPT, JMdict) carries a ShareAlike obligation for derived datasets. Bundling a modified version means releasing that dataset under CC BY-SA. Check whether generated question content counts as an adaptation before mixing it with proprietary content.
- Any "JLPT question dataset" that copies items from the official workbooks or from leaked tests cannot carry a valid open licence, whatever its label says.

### Gaps
- I did not check every GitHub or Hugging Face repository. A small community CC0 or CC BY question set may exist but did not show up in searches.
- The original licence wording on tanos.co.uk could not be checked because the site was unreachable.
